A demonstration of a follow-up tracker and care navigator that runs on written rules and synthetic data. It flags delayed results, missed visits and open handoffs, names who owns the next step and shows when a case is closed. Rules, not AI: every flag comes from a written rule shown with it.

Synthetic demonstration only. No real patients, clinical decisions, live notifications or external services. Dates and deadlines are illustrative workflow data, not clinical guidance. Do not enter personal information.
